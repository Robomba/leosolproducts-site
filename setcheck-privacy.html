2. The short version

Your music never reaches us. SetCheck encrypts your project on your computer before anything

leaves it, and the encrypted file goes to storage you control — or to no cloud at all. We operate

no servers that hold your files, and we have no technical ability to read them.

3. What SetCheck does with your files

  • Encryption happens locally. Your project is packaged and encrypted with AES-256-GCM on your

machine before any upload.

  • We never receive: your project files, the encrypted package, your encryption keys, your PINs,

your storage credentials, your identity private key, your contacts, your settings, your plugin scan

results, or any telemetry. None of it is sent to us, because there is nowhere to send it.

  • Where the encrypted file goes: either (a) a cloud storage bucket you own and configure (for

example your own Cloudflare R2 or Amazon S3 account), or (b) nowhere — "no-cloud mode" leaves an

encrypted file on your disk that you share however you like. We do not control, access, or pay for

that storage.

  • How the recipient opens it. The /open page decrypts the file in the recipient's browser.

The decryption key travels in the URL fragment (the part after #), which by design

(RFC 3986 §3.5) is not transmitted to any

web server — not ours, not anyone's.

  • Your original project is never modified. SetCheck reads it and packages a copy.

The one thing that is not encrypted

Alongside the encrypted package, a small "sidecar" record is stored in your own storage so links

can expire, be revoked, and show a friendly title before decryption. It contains: a random delivery

ID, timestamps and expiry, a download counter, a flag for whether a PIN is required, and the **sender

name and project name you typed**.

Do not type sensitive information into the sender-name or project-name fields.

11. Security

Content is encrypted with AES-256-GCM in authenticated chunks. PIN-protected links additionally

derive keys with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (310,000 iterations). Recipient-locked deliveries use X25519 key

agreement with HKDF-SHA256. Deliveries can be signed so the recipient can verify who sent them.

There is no key escrow. If you lose a link or PIN, that delivery cannot be recovered by anyone,

including us. Report security issues to rosinkai888@gmail.com; see our

security policy and

security.txt.
