Why it exists

A project file is a set of pointers, not the song.

The .als, .flp, or .logicx just points at audio on your drive, plugins you own, and a version of your DAW. Send it raw and your friend opens a session full of grey, silent clips. setcheck packages the whole thing and delivers it so it opens on their machine exactly like it does on yours.

How it works

  • Drop in your project, setcheck checks it and tells you what would break.
  • Pick who gets it, or generate a private link.
  • It’s encrypted on your computer, then uploaded.
  • They click the link and download a project that just works.
  • Trade versions back and forth, only what changed gets sent.

Private by design

Your music, and only your music.

Everything is end-to-end encrypted before it leaves your computer, so it’s readable only by the people you send it to. We keep the encrypted file and nothing we don’t need, no passwords, no listening, no snooping. Even we can’t open your projects. Links expire on their own, and you can kill any link the moment you want it gone.
